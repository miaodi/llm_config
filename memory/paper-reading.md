# Paper Reading Preferences

- Most papers of interest already have a summary or review in Obsidian; look for
  it before creating another summary or starting a discussion from scratch.
- The preferred vault is named `my_obsidian_vault`. Contact-mechanics reviews are
  under `Research/Contact Mechanics`. Resolve the actual vault location from the
  current context or local Obsidian registry rather than assuming an absolute path.
- For paper discussions, explain mathematical analysis through purpose, intuition,
  and intermediate steps, with the original paper as the authority for its claims.
- During paper discussions, proactively save meaningful explanations, insights,
  and verified corrections. This is standing authorization; preserve annotations,
  distinguish discussion-derived ideas from paper claims, and report saved changes.
- Keep one main review per paper. Topic discussions belong under
  `<review-directory>/Discussions/<paper-label>/<topic>.md`, with links from the
  review and back to it. Reuse topic notes; do not merge full discussions into the
  main review unless explicitly requested.
- In finished paper reviews, keep citations and source locators beside claims and
  in proof tables. Omit standalone evidence ledgers and extraction/processing logs;
  state reading coverage and material verification limits once in reading scope.
