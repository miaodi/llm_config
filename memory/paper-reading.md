# Paper Reading Preferences

- Most papers of interest already have a summary or review in Obsidian; look for
  it before creating another summary or starting a discussion from scratch.
- The preferred vault is named `my_obsidian_vault`. Contact-mechanics reviews are
  under `Research/Contact Mechanics`. Resolve the actual vault location from the
  current context or local Obsidian registry rather than assuming an absolute path.
- For paper discussions, explain mathematical analysis through purpose, intuition,
  and intermediate steps, with the original paper as the authority for its claims.
